back when i was working on [Monita Tahalea](https://www.instagram.com/monitatahalea)'s 3rd album, [Dari Balik Jendela](https://open.spotify.com/album/4WLUJgiKTrrQhjDzn6fSsf?si=V-2QgT_8TaeVODcNPyT_qA), specifically on the song [Laila](https://open.spotify.com/track/0pdE5hWLktiP2FrmTP41MC?si=6ff9ca4d492e432e), i was recording a lot of improvisation and sample mangling using [monome norns](https://monome.org/docs/norns/), piano and Monita's voice, like about half an hour of recording.

some of it make it thru, and become the intro part of Laila. but there's still a lot of good stuff in that improvisation recording that i really like. so i decided to scan thru the recording, doing some editing, and released it as single track.
