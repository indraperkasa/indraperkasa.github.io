### technical notes.

* both [M8](https://dirtywave.com/) and [S4](https://torsoelectronics.com/s4) connected to mac desktop using usb. 
* then using [Loopback](https://rogueamoeba.com/loopback/), i route audio from M8 to S4 with details:
  * M8 **Main Out** to S4 **Track 4**
  * M8 **Track 4** (Pads Hypersynth) to S4 **Track 2**
  * M8 **Track 1** (FM Bass Synth) to S4 **Track 3**

* S4 **Track 1** is playing back sample that i also recorded from M8 via usb-Loopback.
  more on this, at first i tried to connect directly using usb from M8 to S4. 
  it worked, but there's audio crackles. 
  i assumed this happen because of the different sample rate. 
  M8 is 44Khz and S4 is 48KHz. 
  so i found a workaround that is working nicely (no crackles) using Loopback as a bridge.  

* M8 also sending midi to S4 to send clock, control Start/Stop, 
  - and send midi cc to control Mix FIlter of S4 Track 1 and 2. 
  - i did this using Midi Patchbay app, its an open source app. very handy app with for this kind of situation, just want a simple routing without needing to use DAW.

* then i use ableton just to record, set audio input to S4, audio out to my audio interface for monitoring.

* its amazing nowadays how you can multitrack record music gear without using audio cables, only 2 usb!
* and just want to say Loopback is really really nice. simple, but very useful.
