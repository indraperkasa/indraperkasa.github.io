### patch notes :

i’m using custom VVOLTT skiff 66hp, with power module IPS2 from seismic industries, powered using powerbank.

i use piezzo tap to the music box, connect the other end to input mutable ears, then output to morphagene, then to mimeophon.
modulation source from antumbra cara (marbles) & nonlinearcircuits hyperdeluxe chaos.

record direct to zoom H5. mastered in DAW and added a little bit of reverb from valhalla supermassive.
