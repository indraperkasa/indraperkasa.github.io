Rupa Suara workshop was held in December last year at GoetheHaus Jakarta, bringing together eight musicians/artists with an interest in immersive audio practices and multimedia composition, co-initiated with Indra Perkasa and Firzi O.

The results of the workshop will be showcased in Festival Alur Bunyi 2024. Audiences can expect to see new mix-media compositions that interact and interrelate, embodying audio-visual practices in a new context that focuses on the experience of hearing and seeing. Reactive visual elements are harmonized with soundscapes that will produce a different experience for visitors to Festival Alur Bunyi.

Rupa Suara are: Alexandra Ronodipuro, Aldo Ahmad Fithra, Bimo Arsa, Dwi Jatmiko, Gabriella Miranda, Kinski Tarlazzi Onggara, Kelvin Djunaidi, Lody Andrian.
