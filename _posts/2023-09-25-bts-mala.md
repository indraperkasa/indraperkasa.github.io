### side technical note:
the shows was done at Graha Bakti Budaya, which has Immersive setup, using [L-acoustics (L-ISA)](https://www.l-acoustics.com/products/l-isa-immersive/) system. 
i believe, this is the first immersive setup in Indonesia at this time of writing.
experiencing for the first time hearing my music thru this immersive setup is so magical, quite an experience. and we got a chance to 'play the music around' is soo muuch fun.
