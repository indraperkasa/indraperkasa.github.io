### patch notes
* sound source is 2 [Tinkle](https://www.nonlinearcircuits.com/modules/p/tinkle) module and [Resonate](https://www.nonlinearcircuits.com/modules/p/resonate).
* master clock is from [Poultry in Motion](https://www.nonlinearcircuits.com/modules/p/poultryinmotion), feed one of the pulse to [Stochaos](https://www.nonlinearcircuits.com/modules/p/stochaos) and [Cellular Automata](https://www.nonlinearcircuits.com/modules/p/cellular-automata). 
* all 8 out from **Tinkle** triggered from **Stochaos**. **Resonate** triggered from **Cellular Automata**.
* some of the sound pass thru [Dispersion Delay](https://www.nonlinearcircuits.com/modules/p/dispersion-delay), modulated by CVs from **Cellular Automata**. 
* the lowest tone of **Tinkle** processed thru [Intimacy](https://www.nonlinearcircuits.com/modules/p/intimacy) (EQ+Drive). 
* reverb is from mixer [Spirit FX8](https://www.soundcraft.com/en/products/spirit-fx8).
