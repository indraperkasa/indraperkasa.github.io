this was a recorded live modular performance at [Kios Ojo Keos](https://www.instagram.com/kiosojokeos/), Jakarta. 6 April 2019.

for this performance i prepared a Sape sample as the center of the composition.

Sape is a traditional string instrument from Borneo (Kalimantan) island, Indonesia.

i want to capture the magical mood of the rain forest thru this performance.
