one day, my wife had an idea to do this beatles cover. so i thought, yeah why not, let’s do that (with a twist). this is my first time doing a cover song, singing together with my wife, and of course with pandemic-singing style.
i hope i don't get too addicted.

the instruments are mostly modular synthesizers, with electric bass and acoustic guitar.

all music arranged, recorded, mixed, & mastered by Lie Indra Perkasa at Gadgadasvara studio.

-----

i asked my best friend John to do the video. at first  i thought it would be just a simple video, nothing fancy, just so that i can have a music video on youtube...

but then John got so excited and came up with this brilliant idea, so we ended up doing 2 full day shoot at home. got another help from my buddy Adi for the shoot. i really love the result of the video, John's vision and imagination really takes this music beyond my expectation.
